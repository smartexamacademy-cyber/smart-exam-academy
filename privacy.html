1. Who we are

Smart Exam Academy is an education app and website for Class 10th, 12th, BA, B.Sc, B.Com and competitive exam students. It is run by Sonu Kumar, Begusarai, Bihar, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app and the website (smartexamacademy-cyber.github.io/smart-exam-academy) collect and how it is used. The app and website use the same student account.

2. What we collect

Account information

  • Sign-in: you sign in with email and password or with your Google account, through Google Firebase Authentication. We do not see or store your password; Firebase handles it. With Google sign-in we receive your name and email address from Google.
  • Name and email address.
  • Mobile number (Indian 10-digit number, asked at sign-up). It is stored in a private part of our database that only you and the app owner can read. It is not shown to other students.
  • Course and semester you choose, so the app can show matching content.
  • Email verification status (whether you entered the correct email OTP, and when).

Courses and purchases

  • Which courses you are enrolled in, when access started and when it ends.
  • For paid courses: the Razorpay payment ID, order ID, amount, the course bought and the time of payment.

Device session

  • To allow one account to be active on only one phone at a time, we save a random session ID, your phone's manufacturer and model name (for example "TECNO SPARK Go"), and the login time.

AI study helper

  • The questions you type in the AI chat, plus the last few messages of the conversation and basic context (your name, course/semester and the courses you have bought), so the answer can be relevant.
  • Microphone (Help AI voice, optional): you can ask the Help AI by speaking. The microphone is used only while the app is open on your screen and only when you tap the mic button or have turned on the opt-in "AI को नाम से बुलाएँ" (call the AI by name, "Help") switch in Profile; it is off during videos and running tests, and it stops when you leave the app. Your speech is converted to text by your phone's Google speech recognizer (Google's own terms apply). We do not record, store or upload any audio; only the recognized text of your question is sent to the AI, like a typed question. You can deny or remove the microphone permission at any time and still type your questions.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, SMS or call logs, and we never store microphone audio. The app asks only for internet access, (on Android 13+) permission to show notifications, and, only if you use voice questions, microphone permission.

3. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, verify your email and keep fake accounts out.
  • To show you the right courses and to unlock the paid courses you bought, for their validity period.
  • To confirm payments and help you if a payment or unlock has a problem.
  • To answer your study questions in the AI chat.
  • To send study updates and notices.
  • To contact you about your account or a support request you made.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not build marketing profiles.

4. Services that process data for us

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Messaging, App Check): stores your account and course data, sends push notifications and helps block fake app traffic. Firebase privacy.
  • Razorpay: processes all payments. You enter card, UPI or net-banking details directly in Razorpay's checkout. The app never sees or stores your card number, UPI PIN or bank details. Razorpay privacy.
  • Cloudflare Workers (our small server) and Brevo: when you verify your email, our server on Cloudflare creates a 6-digit OTP and sends it to your email through Brevo. Only a scrambled (hashed) copy of the OTP is kept, for up to 10 minutes. The same server confirms Razorpay payments to unlock your course instantly.
  • Google Gemini: your AI chat questions go through our server to Google's Gemini AI to create an answer. We do not keep your chat on our server after the answer is returned; the conversation stays on your phone only for the current session. There is a daily limit on AI questions per student. Google's own terms apply to how Gemini handles requests.
  • Online voice service: the AI can read its answers aloud. It first uses your phone's own text-to-speech voice. If your phone has no suitable (for example Hindi) voice, a short piece of the answer text (up to about 220 characters) is sent through our server to an online text-to-speech service (Google) to make the audio. No personal account details are sent with it.
  • YouTube and Google Drive: video classes play from YouTube and some PDFs open from Google Drive, so those services may receive normal technical information (like IP address) under their own policies.

5. Stored on your device

  • PDF notes you download are saved on your phone (Downloads/Smart Exam Academy and the app's Downloads screen). We do not upload them anywhere. You can delete them from your phone at any time.
  • The app saves small settings on your phone, such as your chosen course/semester, the voice on/off setting and how many AI questions you used today.
  • Screenshots and screen recording are blocked inside the app to protect course content.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data, and the app shows no ads. Your data is shared only with the service providers listed above, only as far as needed to run the app, or if Indian law requires us to share it.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules allow each student to read only their own record; your mobile number, enrollments and payments are not visible to other students. Paid course content opens only for students with an active enrollment. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • We keep your account data while your account exists.
  • Payment records may be kept as long as needed for tax, accounting and refund purposes required by law, even after the account is deleted.
  • Email OTP codes are deleted automatically within about 10 minutes.

To delete your account and data, email sonukumarhark@gmail.com from your registered email, or call/WhatsApp +91 7009466362, with your registered email address. We will delete your account, profile, mobile number and enrollments within 30 days and confirm to you. Deleting your account removes access to purchased courses.

9. Students under 18

Many of our students are school and college students, and some may be under 18. If you are under 18, please use the app with the knowledge and consent of a parent or guardian, especially before making any payment. A parent or guardian can contact us to see or delete a child's data. We collect only what is needed to run the account and courses.

10. Your choices

  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it (see section 8).
  • You can turn off notifications in your phone settings at any time.
  • You can choose not to use the AI chat or the voice feature.
  • The "call the AI by name" switch is off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off in Profile or remove the microphone permission in your phone settings at any time.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it uses data, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be announced in the app.
